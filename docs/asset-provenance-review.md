> Status: **preliminary documentation, not a rights clearance or a complete asset audit**.
> Prepared November 6, 2026 from the independent publication review of repository history.

## Creative workflow and scope

The project author has described TOGETHER! as an original game concept developed with ChatGPT and AI-assisted creative/development workflows. Existing project documentation also identifies AI-generated artwork for the original Tiny Heroes worlds and for Desert/Ocean.

This high-level account does **not**, by itself, identify the exact generation session, references, editing process, or license of each asset listed below. A commit adding a file is evidence of repository history, not proof of the underlying image's origin.

The publication review examined 12 branches, 165 reachable commits, 638 unique historical text blob versions, and the then-current asset tree. It reported no confirmed embedded credentials in its covered text/history, but this is not a guarantee about unexamined sources, binary metadata or future commits.

## How to close each record

For each row, document:

1. **Origin:** original hand-drawn source, AI generation, AI-assisted composite, or third-party source (including tool/provider, when known).
2. **Inputs:** whether any third-party images, characters, logos, photos, or other copyrighted reference assets were used.
3. **Edits:** relevant human composition, correction, layout and export work, if applicable.
4. **Rights:** permission to include and redistribute the material; preserve any third-party license and attribution.
5. **Evidence:** link to a retained source/generation record or a dated author attestation, without publishing private account information.

If a file includes external material without adequate permissions, replace it before changing the repository visibility. Do not assert exclusive copyright in wholly AI-generated material where the applicable law may not recognize such copyright.

## Release boundary

This inventory is a transparent record of **open verification items**, not a declaration that those items have been cleared. Publishing the repository would expose historical branches and accessible revisions as well as `main`.

Before a visibility change, re-check the repository's current HEAD and reachable history for credentials and private information. The audit's checked `main` was `36348f19aa2657222a1b6de0e58199399728b6a5`, and subsequent commits require a delta review.
