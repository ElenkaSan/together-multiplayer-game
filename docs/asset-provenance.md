# Asset provenance record

## Maintainer declaration — 2026-11-06

Elena Nurullina confirmed that the game's visual concepts and materials were
created from her own ideas through ChatGPT, rather than downloaded from other
games, stock libraries or third-party asset packs. This confirmation covers
the project materials listed below, including the previously undocumented
maps, atlases, social previews, icons and the historical homepage background.

This is a maintainer-supplied provenance declaration, not an independent legal
verification, a generation receipt or a claim that every AI-generated element
has exclusive copyright protection. Existing repository documentation also
describes built-in image generation for the initial Forest/animal/world art
and Desert/Ocean art.

Source-code licensing and non-code asset permissions remain as described in
[ASSETS.md](../ASSETS.md) and [COPYRIGHT.md](../COPYRIGHT.md). The declaration does
not change third-party dependency or font licenses and does not create a new
asset-reuse license.
